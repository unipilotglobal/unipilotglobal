Cancelling or rescheduling a session

You can ask to cancel or reschedule a paid session at least hours before it starts. We will normally accept requests made in time. If less than hours remain, a full refund may not apply.

When we cancel or cannot deliver

If your pilot does not attend, or the session cannot take place because of a platform problem, a technical fault or another reason within our control, you can choose a full refund, a new time, or an equivalent service.

How refunds are processed

Approved refunds are sent for processing within business days. The time it takes to reach your account also depends on your bank, mobile financial service or payment provider.

Digital guidelines and materials

Digital guidelines are delivered online right after payment. Because they are delivered immediately, they are generally not refundable once delivered, unless they are faulty or not as described. Free materials have no charge.
