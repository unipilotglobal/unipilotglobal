What we collect

  • Information you send us in forms: name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, country, study goals, grades, test scores, the scholarships you are interested in, and any message or document link you add.
  • Booking and payment details, such as the service chosen, the pilot, the time and a payment reference. We do not store your card number.

A share ends by itself after a set time (30 days unless you choose otherwise), and you can stop it at any time.
  • Messages you send us by email or WhatsApp.
  • Reviews and stories you choose to send, with your name, country and photo if you provide them.
  • Basic technical data that our hosting and the services below receive when you visit, such as your IP address and browser type.

Why we use it

  • To answer your request, book and deliver your session, and give you the guidance you asked for.
  • To send you confirmations and replies.
  • To check payments, prevent fraud and misuse, and keep records of our service.
  • To improve our website and materials.
  • To publish your review, only after your permission and our check.

Where the law requires a legal basis, we rely on your consent, on the need to provide the service you asked for, on our legitimate interest in running a safe and useful service, and on legal obligations.

Who receives your data

We do not sell your personal data. We share limited data only with providers that help us run the service, and only as needed:

  • Email and form delivery: what you send in a form goes to our own form service (a server function at our database provider, Supabase). It checks that a person, not a robot, sent it, keeps a copy for up to 180 days so that nothing is lost (together with a one-way anonymous code made from your internet address, only to recognise robots), and e-mails it to our inbox through Resend. If our form service is not available, a backup form delivery service (FormSubmit, and possibly Web3Forms or our own Google script) may be used instead.
  • Cloudflare: we use Cloudflare to deliver and protect this website and to tell robots from people when you sign up or send a form (Cloudflare Turnstile). Cloudflare sees technical data of your visit, such as your internet address.
  • Account database and sign-in: Supabase, which stores your account data and runs sign-in for us (in a data centre in Europe).
  • Website hosting for this site.
  • YouTube, which loads videos only after you click play, using YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode. Our text fonts are stored on our own site, so your browser does not contact Google for them.
  • WhatsApp, if you choose to message us there. The button may pass through a forwarding page of ours that stores nothing about you except a daily count per anonymous code, to stop robots. WhatsApp's own policy then applies.
  • Card payments: Stripe, which takes your card payment. You enter your card details on Stripe's own page, so we never see or store them. We receive only a payment reference and the amount.
  • Payments by bKash, Nagad, Rocket or bank transfer: you send the money yourself from your own app to our official account. We ask you for the transaction ID, the mobile number (or bank and account holder) you sent it from and the amount, so our team can check the payment in our account. We store these details with the payment record, and we erase the number you sent from when you delete your account. We never ask for your PIN or password, and nobody at UniPilot Global will ever ask you for them.
  • Taka prices: the small "about ৳" amounts use public exchange rates from Exchange Rate API. They are for information; the exact amount you must send is shown, and saved, when you pay.
  • Emails about your account and sessions (confirmations and reminders): Resend, which sends these emails for us.
  • The optional "Ask our AI helper" chat: if you use it, the text you type, and a short list of matching scholarships from this site, are sent to Anthropic, which runs the AI model, to write the answer. We do not store your questions. Anthropic's own policy applies to what it receives. Please do not type personal or sensitive details into the chat.
  • Public authorities, where the law requires it.

Some of these providers are located outside your country, so your data may be processed abroad. We choose providers that protect data appropriately.

Storage on your device
