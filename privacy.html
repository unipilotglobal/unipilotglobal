Privacy

What we collect

When you send a consultation request or join deadline alerts we collect the details you type into the form, such as your name, email, phone number, citizenship, academic background and your message.

We use your details only to reply to you, provide the service you asked for and send updates you signed up for. We do not sell your information.

Requests are stored in our private Google account and emailed to our team. Our website may use Google Fonts and YouTube to show fonts and videos, which can set their own cookies.
