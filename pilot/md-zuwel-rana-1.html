About Md

I am Md. Zuwel Rana, a PhD researcher in Chemistry at Université de Montréal, Canada, and a former Erasmus Mundus Joint Master's Scholar. I completed my BSc and MSc in Chemistry at Pabna University of Science and Technology, Bangladesh, followed by an Erasmus Mundus Joint Master's degree (SERP+) at Université Paris-Saclay, France, and the University of Genoa, Italy. My academic journey has given me valuable experience in international scholarship applications, graduate admissions, research opportunities, and adapting to different academic environments. My research interests include materials chemistry, organic and polymer synthesis, photochemistry, photocatalysis, and advanced functional materials. I am passionate about helping students pursue higher education abroad, particularly through fully funded Master's and PhD opportunities. I can provide guidance on scholarship searches, SOP and CV preparation, contacting professors, research proposals, interviews, and university selection.
