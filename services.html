Visa and travel

Visa guidance for your student journey

Choose your destination to see the typical processing time, key documents and the official link. Always confirm everything on the official site.

The 7 steps from offer letter to arrival
  1. Get your admission or scholarship letter.
  2. Read the student visa rules on the official embassy or government site.
  3. Collect your documents and certified translations.
  4. Book your visa appointment as early as possible.
  5. Pay the fee, attend the appointment and submit your documents.
  6. After approval, arrange accommodation, insurance and travel.
  7. On arrival, register your address and keep your documents together.
