UNIPILOT GLOBAL - website update 3 (29 October 2026)
Upload this AFTER update 2 (it builds on it).

WHAT CHANGED
1. COMPUTER MENU: on a computer the full menu is always shown in one clean row. The hamburger (three lines) now only
   appears on phones and small tablets. "Home" is reached through the logo on a computer.
2. NO SEPARATE "BOOK A CONSULTATION". The consultation page became the PILOTS page (pilots.html).
   Every old button now says "Find your pilot" or "Get matched with a pilot". Old links to consultation.html still
   work: they forward to the Pilots page automatically.
3. SHORT REQUEST FORM. The long form is now "Request a session" with 5 required answers (name, email, WhatsApp/phone,
   what you need, degree). Everything else (country, GPA, tests, budget, documents...) is optional and tucked under
   "Add more details (optional)". Reason: shorter first-contact forms get more enquiries, and the full details will
   later live in the student's profile so nobody types them twice.
4. NEW "CONTACT" PAGE (contact.html) and a "Contact" item in the menu. It is for students who did not find what they
   were looking for: topic, name, email, message, optional WhatsApp, plus WhatsApp/email buttons, quick links
   and 4 quick answers. Messages arrive in your inbox with the subject "New message: ...".
5. CLEANER TOP BAR. It now reads: [FREE SEMINAR] Seminar title - date - Reserve your seat. When no seminar is planned it
   says: "Free scholarship and study-abroad guidance for students worldwide". On phones it is one short line.

HOW TO UPLOAD
1. Unzip. Copy the files into your website folder, choose "Replace". Files inside "assets" go into your assets folder.
2. GitHub Desktop: summary "Pilots page, Contact page, desktop menu", Commit, Push.
3. Wait 1 to 2 minutes. Refresh with Ctrl+Shift+R (computer) or open in a private tab (phone).
