HOW TO UPLOAD
1. Unzip. Copy the files into your website folder, choose "Replace". Files inside "assets" go into your assets folder.
2. GitHub Desktop: write a summary like "Visible sign in, phone design", click Commit, then Push.
3. Wait 1 to 2 minutes. On the phone, close the tab and open the site again (or open it in a private tab).

TWO SWITCHES IN accounts.js
  CONFIG.accountsLaunch = true;    change to false to hide Sign in and Save again.
  CONFIG.googleSignIn   = false;   change to true ONLY after Google is set up in Supabase.

IMPORTANT BEFORE YOU ADVERTISE SIGN-UP
Supabase's built-in email service only sends to your own team and is very limited. If "Confirm email" is ON in Supabase,
new visitors may see "We could not send the email right now". Two ways to fix it (I will guide you):
  a) Quick: Supabase, Authentication, Sign In / Providers, Email: switch "Confirm email" OFF. Sign-up then works at once.
  b) Better, later: connect a free email sender (Brevo or Resend) and keep "Confirm email" ON.
Google sign-in does not need email, so it works either way.
