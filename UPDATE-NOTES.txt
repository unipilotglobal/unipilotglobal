3. In GitHub Desktop you will see the changed files. Write a summary such as "Accounts (private test), October update".
   Click Commit, then Push.

TESTING YOUR ACCOUNT (private test mode)
- Open https://unipilotglobal.com/account.html and sign in with the email and password you created in Supabase.
- You should see "Hello, ..." with an Admin and a Finance badge.
- Visitors cannot see the Sign in link until you launch. To launch: open accounts.js, change
      CONFIG.accountsLaunch = false;   to   CONFIG.accountsLaunch = true;
  upload, and the Sign in link and the Save button on scholarship pages appear for everyone.
- Do NOT launch before email sending (SMTP) is set up in Supabase. Until then, new people cannot receive their
  confirmation email. I will guide you through it.

THE ONE SETTINGS FILE FOR ACCOUNTS: accounts.js
It holds your Supabase address and the PUBLIC key. Both are safe in a website. Never put the "secret" or
"service_role" key or the database password in any website file.
