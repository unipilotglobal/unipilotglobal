The process

Your visa journey in 7 steps

Hold your offer

Most visa offices want proof of admission or a scholarship offer letter first.

Find the right visa

Open the official embassy or immigration site of your destination and find the student or study visa.

Prove your funding

Use your scholarship award letter or bank statements, and check how much the country expects.

Collect documents

Passport, photos, transcripts, language results, insurance and any medical or police certificates asked for.

Book and attend

Book the embassy or visa-centre appointment, pay the fee, and attend biometrics or the interview.

Wait for the decision

Track your application. Avoid non-refundable flights until the visa is approved, unless advised otherwise.

Prepare to travel

Insurance, accommodation, flights, arrival documents and university orientation.

Common mistakes to avoid

Applying too late

Processing times vary a lot. Start as soon as you hold your offer.

Inconsistent details

Names, dates and funding must match across every document and form.

Unclear funding

Show exactly who pays for tuition and living costs, with proof.

Missing translations

Documents not in the official language often need certified translations.

Weak interview answers

Know your programme, your funding and your plan to return or progress.

Unofficial websites

Only pay fees through the official government or embassy channels.
