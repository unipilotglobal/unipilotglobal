About this opportunity

The International Program Associate scheme lets non-Japanese doctoral students from partner universities carry out their PhD research at RIKEN, Japan's largest research institution, for one to three years with a living allowance and housing support.

Tips from our advisors

  1. Read the eligibility line by line before you write anything. Most rejections are eligibility mismatches, not weak essays.
  2. Contact potential supervisors before you apply. Send a one-page research summary and a short, specific email.
  3. Start at least 8 to 12 weeks early. Certified transcripts, translations and referee letters usually take longer than expected.
  4. Ask your referees at least 4 weeks ahead and give them your CV, your draft SOP and the deadline.
  5. Submit at least 48 hours before the deadline. Portals slow down on the last day.
  6. Have your SOP and CV reviewed by someone who has seen successful applications before you submit.
